# Trials Ascension — Development Changelog

## V9H — Mythic Loot Exclusive

- Restricted Mythic weapons to the dedicated Mythic reward pool.
- Mythic weapon pool: Hallebarde du Zénith, Trident de la Marée Funèbre, Faux de Myrkul, Arc du Néant de Dakousai.
- Standardized high-tier prices: Very Rare/Epic 900 gp, Legendary 1,800 gp, Mythic 3,000 gp.
- Preserved the V9G native-weapon corrections for Dakousai and Yurgir.
- Arc du Néant de Dakousai uses the native Bow of the Banshee weapon base/behaviour, retains its custom spells/bonuses and has permanent Necrotic VFX.
- Fléau du Vortex: removed problematic Electrified Flail and Thunderous Smite; added native Concussive Smash and Weakening Strike.
- KatanaTheus: removed duplicate displayed +2 Dexterity line.
- Faux de Myrkul: removed the non-functional Destructive Wave: Necrotic grant.
- Removed Creuset de l'Alchimiste and Espadon du Guetteur from the active custom-weapon set.
- FR/EN/ZH localization maintained for current development content. Full Chinese localization remains under global audit before a 100% claim.

### Development note

V9H is a test/development branch. The repository's public GPL source snapshot remains version 2.3.4.27 until the next public source release is prepared.
